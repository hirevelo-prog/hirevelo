# HireVelo Technologies

The official website of **HireVelo Technologies**, a modern recruitment services company helping businesses connect with relevant, well-matched talent.

## About

HireVelo works with businesses to understand their hiring requirements, identify relevant professionals, screen for role suitability, and connect businesses with the right talent.

**The Right Person. For The Right Role.**

## Services

- Permanent Recruitment
- Specialist Recruitment
- Volume Hiring
- Executive & Critical Hiring

## Website

https://hirevelotech.com

## Core Approach

Understand → Source → Screen → Match → Connect → Hire

## Tech & Design

The website is designed as a premium, responsive B2B recruitment experience with:

- Modern HireVelo visual identity
- Purple, white and dark brand system
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Recruitment-focused visual animations
- Scroll-driven interactions
- Accessible and performance-conscious frontend
- Employer-focused recruitment enquiry flow

HireVelo is **not a job portal**. The website is designed primarily for businesses looking for recruitment support.
